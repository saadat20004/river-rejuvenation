Arriving

  • Check in after 2 PM, check out before 11 AM. Early or late times may be possible, just ask.
  • Cindy meets you when you arrive and shows you around.
  • 2WD accessible. The entrance is tight, so take longer rigs in slowly.
  • Park right at the site. Room for up to 10 vehicles.
  • The exact address is shared after you book.

Good to know

  • Ground is loose gravel and needs some leveling, though there are flat spots.
  • A family campsite next door is used on summer weekends only, behind a privacy fence.
  • No electric or sewer hookups. Generators are fine day or night.
  • Pack out your own trash. Heavy-duty bags are provided.
  • Strict cancellation policy: cancel 21+ days ahead for a full refund.
